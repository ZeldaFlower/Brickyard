# Brickyard

The Brickyard Restaurant in Wrenshall was built by the community to honor the town's history as a major brick-producing center, which began with the discovery of clay by Mr. Habhegger in 1883. The restaurant itself is constructed with bricks from the local brickyards, and its name reflects the industry that brought the town into being. After years of local fundraising and labor, the restaurant opened in 1990 as a central gathering place for the community, a vision shared by three local gentlemen. 
* Discovery of clay: In 1883, Mr. Habhegger discovered high-quality brick clay in the area, leading to the establishment of the first brickyard.
* Industrial boom: At its peak, the brickyard industry was booming, with as many as six brickyards operating in the area, at one point producing around 4 million bricks per year and employing 35 men.
* Community-driven project: The Brickyard Restaurant was built in 1990 through community fundraising and volunteer work by residents who wanted a local spot for coffee and socializing.
* Local history: The building itself is constructed with the iconic yellow bricks from the original brickyards, just like the First Presbyterian Church.
* A lasting tribute: The restaurant's name is a direct nod to the brick industry that was the lifeblood of Wrenshall for decades, and the town continues to celebrate this legacy with its annual Brickyard Days festival.
